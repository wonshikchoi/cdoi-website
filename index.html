About the Center

Solving the inverse scattering problem, one wavefront at a time.

Our center develops optical methodologies for imaging, sensing, and light manipulation of targets located deep within scattering media — beyond the working depth of conventional approaches.

We solve inverse-scattering problems at the microscopic level of scattering events and investigate the physics of wave propagation inside complex media. Our methods are applied to biological and biomedical problems, as well as the inspection of stacked semiconductors such as HBM.

Established August 2024, funded by the Global Leader Project of the National Research Foundation of Korea.

Research Directions
R / 01

Deep Imaging

Reflection-matrix imaging and adaptive-optical microscopy for label-free, aberration-free imaging through intact biological tissues.
R / 02

Holographic Endoscopy

Lensless single-fiber endoscopes and matrix approaches enabling microscopic imaging in confined geometries.
R / 03

Optical Physics

Wave propagation through disordered media, transmission eigenchannels, and the physics of multiple scattering.
