A team of physicists, optical engineers, and computational scientists working across scales — from single photons in scattering media to volumetric imaging of living tissue.

Director · Est. 2024
— Principal Investigator

Wonshik Choi

Professor of Physics · Associate Director, IBS CMSD

Wonshik Choi leads the Center for Deep Optical Imaging at Korea University. His research develops reflection-matrix imaging, adaptive optics, and holographic endoscopy to see deep into scattering media — opening new windows into biological tissue and nanoscale materials.

His group has pioneered the accumulation of single-scattered waves, time-reversal matrix methods, and lensless fiber endoscopes. Their work has appeared in Nature Photonics, Nature Physics, and Nature Reviews Physics.
